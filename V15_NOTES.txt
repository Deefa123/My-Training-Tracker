My Training Tracker — V15

New in V15:
- Daily bodyweight logging remains available from the Workout tab.
- Weekly bodyweight averages are calculated Monday–Sunday.
- A weekly average uses only the days actually logged, so missed weigh-ins are ignored rather than treated as zero or missing weight.
- This week is compared with the previous calendar week to show weight gained/lost.
- Weekly history shows each recorded week, its average, days logged, and week-on-week change.
- Existing V14 workout, history, progress, PR, AI Coach, settings, theme, and backup functionality is retained.
- Bodyweight data continues to use the existing localStorage key for compatibility with V14.
