V24.3 — Explicit starting value for goals

- Bodyweight goals now ask for Starting weight and Target weight.
- Weight-loss progress is calculated from Start -> Current -> Target.
- Example: 90 kg start, 86 kg current, 75 kg target = 26.7% complete.
- Weight-gain goals can also use an explicit starting weight.
- Measurement goals also support an explicit starting measurement.
- Goal cards display Start, Current and Target.
- V24.2 exercise-goal percentage logic retained.
