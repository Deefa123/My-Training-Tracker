V24.2 — Goal progress percentage fix

- Upward goals now show intuitive absolute progress toward the target.
- Example: current bench best 62.5 kg toward a 100 kg goal = 62.5%.
- Exercise load, estimated 1RM and upward measurement goals use current / target.
- Downward goals continue to use progress from the value recorded when the goal was created toward the lower target.
- Existing V24.1 goal-form fixes retained.
