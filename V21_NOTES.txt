V21 — Smarter Coach

Training intelligence:
- Reviews up to the last 5 sessions per exercise instead of only the latest session.
- Recognises repeated rep progression at the same load as genuine progressive overload.
- Distinguishes a one-off poor session from repeated under-performance.
- Flags a plateau only after several sessions remain essentially flat.
- Avoids automatically reducing load after one weak workout.
- Uses Cut / Maintain / Bulk phase context when interpreting performance.
- During a cut, maintaining strength is treated as a positive outcome.
- During a bulk, prolonged stalls receive more scrutiny.
- Adds "Why" explanations to every next-session recommendation.
- Adds a Smarter Coach Insights section for plateaus, one-off dips and rep progression.
- All exercises from the latest logged workout remain included.

Existing V20 unified dashboard, bodyweight coaching, History, Progress, PRs, themes and saved-data structure retained.
