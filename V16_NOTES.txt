V16 — Adaptive Cut / Maintain / Bulk bodyweight coach

Cut:
- Target: -0.50% to -0.75% per week
- 3-week average trend
- Proportional calorie adjustment if outside target

Maintain:
- Captures current weight as maintenance reference when selected
- Keeps weekly average within +/-0.5%
- Proportional calorie adjustment if drifting above/below range

Bulk:
- <=80 kg: +0.15% to +0.25% per week
- >80 to 85 kg: +0.10% to +0.15% per week
- >85 kg: +0.05% to +0.15% per week

Calories:
- Approximation uses 7,700 kcal per kg
- Rounded to nearest 25 kcal/day
- Capped between 50 and 350 kcal/day
- Reassess after another 3 weeks

Missed weigh-ins are excluded from weekly averages.
Existing V15 features retained.
